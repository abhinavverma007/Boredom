📘 How to Play

• You see a 3 × 3 grid with numbers from 1 to 9.

• Click on any number x to collect points.

• Your score increases by:
x × (how many times x appears)

Example:
If you click on 3 and there are three 3s on the grid,
your score increases by 3 × 3 = 9 points.

• After clicking x, all cells containing:
x, x − 1, and x + 1 are removed from the grid.

Example:
If you click on 3, then all 2, 3, and 4 tiles will disappear.

• The game ends when the grid becomes empty.

• Try to reach the optimal score using smart choices!
